## Evidence

The July 2025 investigation describes patient-safety risks when discharge information is incomplete, delayed, sent to the wrong destination or difficult for primary-care staff to process. It treats electronic discharge communication as a cross-organisational workflow rather than a single transmission event.

## Modelling use and caveats

The investigation is highly relevant to failure modes but does not provide a transferable transition probability for the synthetic world. Keep the measured NHS-SIM letter-routing values as the direct calibration.

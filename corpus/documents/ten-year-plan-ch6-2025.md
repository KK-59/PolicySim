## Evidence

Chapter 6 commits to greater publication of quality measures and more systematic, comparable collection of patient-reported outcome measures and patient-reported experience measures.

## Modelling use and caveats

This source helps define reportable outcomes and auditability. It does not provide a model input or a numerical causal effect.

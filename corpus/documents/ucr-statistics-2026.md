- Evidence kind: measured
- Applicability: supporting

## Evidence

The published collection reports the percentage of urgent community response referrals receiving a response within 2 hours. The current national expectation is at least 70%.

## Modelling use and caveats

The publication warns that figures before and after the April 2023 definition change are not directly comparable. This is an observed service-level output, not a capacity multiplier.

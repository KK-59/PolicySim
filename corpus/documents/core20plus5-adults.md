## Evidence

Core20PLUS5 defines a population-health approach centred on the most deprived 20% of the national population, locally selected PLUS groups and five clinical areas requiring accelerated improvement.

## Modelling use and caveats

This is a stratification and equity framework, not an effect-size source. Use it to require subgroup reporting and avoid assuming an average benefit is equally distributed.

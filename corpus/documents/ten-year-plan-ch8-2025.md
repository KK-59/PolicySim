## Evidence

Chapter 8 identifies advanced wearables and biomarkers as technologies intended to support reductions in premature cardiovascular mortality and describes connecting wearable and biosensor data to the NHS App and neighbourhood teams.

## Modelling use and caveats

This is policy direction rather than comparative effectiveness evidence. It supports the existence of a monitoring lever but not its numerical value or outcome multiplier.

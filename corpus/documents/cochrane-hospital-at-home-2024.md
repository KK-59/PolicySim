# Admission avoidance hospital at home

- ID: cochrane-hospital-at-home-2024
- Publisher: Cochrane
- Year: 2024
- Source: https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD007491_hospital-home-services-avoid-admission-hospital
- Parameters: levers.hospitalToCommunityShare
- Range or CI: yes
- Evidence kind: literature
- Applicability: supporting

## Evidence

Across 20 randomised trials with 3,100 participants, hospital at home had mortality RR 0.88 (95% CI 0.68 to 1.13), readmission RR 1.14 (0.97 to 1.34), and residential-care RR 0.53 (0.41 to 0.69) in older populations. Hospital length of stay ranged from 4.1 to 18.5 days for hospital care and 1.2 to 5.1 days for the hospital-at-home groups; total hospital-at-home treatment ranged from 3 to 20.7 days.

## Modelling use and caveats

These estimates apply to selected patients receiving a staffed clinical service, not to an arbitrary increase in community routing. Eligibility, escalation and programme capacity must be represented before applying an outcome effect.

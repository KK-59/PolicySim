# Variation in general-practice consultation length

- ID: gp-consultation-variation-1999
- Publisher: British Journal of General Practice
- Year: 1999
- Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10187199/
- Parameters: serviceTimes.gpConsultation
- Range or CI: yes
- Evidence kind: literature
- Applicability: direct

## Evidence

Across 836 consultations, mean consultation duration varied from 5.7 to 8.5 minutes between practices and from 4.4 to 11 minutes between individual GPs.

## Modelling use and caveats

This is old observational UK evidence and consultation modes have changed. It is still a defensible broad range when the local simulator provides no GP consultation-time observation, but should be superseded by newer matched data.

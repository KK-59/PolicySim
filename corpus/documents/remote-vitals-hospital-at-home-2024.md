# Remote vital-sign monitoring in admission-avoidance hospital at home

## Evidence

A systematic review of 21 randomised trials with 3,459 participants found mortality RR 0.94 (95% CI 0.78 to 1.12), readmission RR 1.02 (0.77 to 1.35), and length-of-stay mean difference 1.91 days (0.71 to 3.12) for hospital at home compared with hospital care. Reporting was insufficient to classify the monitoring approach in most studies; the automated-monitoring subgroup had readmission RR 0.30 (0.11 to 0.86).

## Modelling use and caveats

The overall intervals include little or no effect and the favourable automated subgroup is based on limited evidence. This is useful counterevidence to an automatic monitoring benefit, but it does not estimate a dose-response for monitoring frequency or a patient-routing probability.

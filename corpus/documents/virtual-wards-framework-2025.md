## Evidence

The framework expects virtual wards to maintain occupancy consistently above 80% while providing hospital-level care at home with defined clinical governance and escalation.

## Modelling use and caveats

The occupancy threshold is an operating target, not an effect size. It can validate utilisation outputs but must not be substituted for monitoring intensity or routing share.

## Evidence

Chapter 2 commits to a Neighbourhood Health Service, care planning for people with complex needs, neighbourhood centres operating at least 12 hours a day on 6 days a week, and at least doubling personal health budgets by 2028 to 2029.

## Modelling use and caveats

These are national policy commitments and operating-model directions, not effect estimates. They justify policy scenarios but do not identify a numerical community-capacity multiplier or hospital-to-community routing share.

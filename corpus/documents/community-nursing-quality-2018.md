## Evidence

Observed community-nursing visits ranged from 5 to 95 minutes. Average visits were 35 minutes for senior or specialist community nurses and 23 minutes for band 5 district nurses.

## Modelling use and caveats

The observed range is genuine but combines heterogeneous visit purposes and staff grades. It should inform sensitivity or validation, not silently replace NHS-SIM's documented constant 90-minute generic community visit.

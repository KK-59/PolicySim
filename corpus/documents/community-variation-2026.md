## Evidence

The guidance reports substantial unwarranted variation across community services, including an example of roughly five-fold staffing variation between services.

## Modelling use and caveats

This demonstrates that a single national point estimate is unsafe. It does not reveal the local site's slot capacity or visit duration, so use it to widen sensitivity analysis rather than as a direct calibration.

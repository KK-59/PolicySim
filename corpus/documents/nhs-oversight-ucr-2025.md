## Evidence

The oversight metric defines performance as qualifying two-hour urgent community response referrals responded to within 2 hours divided by all qualifying referrals in the reporting period.

## Modelling use and caveats

Use this definition to label and calculate an output consistently. It supplies neither a capacity value nor a causal effect.

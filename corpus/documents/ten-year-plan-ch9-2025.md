## Evidence

Chapter 9 proposes year-of-care payments spanning primary, community, mental-health, outpatient, emergency and inpatient care, with pioneer work beginning in financial year 2026 to 2027. It states that implementation depends on reliable cost and activity data.

## Modelling use and caveats

This supports cross-service policy design and outcome-based incentives. It does not identify patient-flow probabilities and should not be used as a routing-share prior.

## Evidence

The workforce standards use 9 to 10 visits per day as a planning range and state that a scheduled visit should allow at least 30 minutes, excluding travel.

## Modelling use and caveats

These are professional planning standards for district nursing, not measured throughput for every community-care pathway. Travel, acuity and skill mix remain material.

## Evidence

The plan says that by 2028 patients should be able to communicate with care professionals, view and draft care plans, and book or hold appointments through the NHS App. It also estimates that saving 90 seconds per GP appointment could release more than 2,000 full-time-equivalent staff capacity.

## Modelling use and caveats

The 90-second claim is a productivity illustration, not a measured consultation-time distribution. The chapter supports digital-access scenarios but does not set a telephone follow-up share or monitoring effect.

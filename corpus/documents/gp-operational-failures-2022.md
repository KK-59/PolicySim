# Identifying how GPs spend their time and the obstacles they face

- ID: gp-operational-failures-2022
- Publisher: British Journal of General Practice
- Year: 2022
- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8813099/
- Parameters: capacities.gpAdminShare, serviceTimes.gpConsultation
- Range or CI: yes
- Evidence kind: literature
- Applicability: supporting

## Evidence

A time-and-motion study covering 7,679 GP tasks and 238 hours across 61 clinical sessions estimated operational failures at 5.0% of tasks (95% CI 4.5% to 5.4%) and 3.9% of clinical time (95% CI 3.2% to 4.5%).

## Modelling use and caveats

Operational-failure time is not equivalent to all GP administration. Use this interval for a waste or recoverable-capacity scenario, not as gpAdminShare itself.

## Evidence

The analysis reports 29,364 full-time-equivalent GPs in September 2015 and 27,200 in May 2023, a reduction of 2,164, alongside community workforce shortages and rising demand.

## Modelling use and caveats

This historical national workforce change shows capacity pressure but does not convert directly into sessions or slots for one synthetic site. Use it as scenario context, not local calibration.

- Range or CI: no
- Evidence kind: documented
- Applicability: boundary

## Evidence

The statutory guidance structures discharge around Home First and pathways 0 to 3, from no formal post-discharge support through increasingly intensive home, bed-based and long-term support. It describes transfer-of-care hubs coordinating health, social care, housing and voluntary-sector input.

## Modelling use and caveats

Pathway numbers are categories, not probabilities. This source defines valid discharge mechanisms but does not supply the share of people suitable for community routing in the simulator.

# Community health services two-hour urgent community response standard

## Evidence

The national urgent community response standard is framed around a 2-hour response for eligible crisis-response referrals. The standard concerns elapsed time from referral to response, not the duration of the clinical visit.

## Modelling use and caveats

Do not map 120 minutes to community visit service time. It is an access standard and is useful for validating a waiting-time output or service-level constraint.

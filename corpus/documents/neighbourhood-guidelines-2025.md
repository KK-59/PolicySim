## Evidence

The guidance links neighbourhood services with integrated intermediate care, Home First, step-up and step-down care, urgent neighbourhood services and community diagnostic capacity. It notes a national footprint of 170 community diagnostic centres by March 2025.

## Modelling use and caveats

This is service-design evidence. It names mechanisms available to a policy but does not quantify a transfer probability or patient-level effect.

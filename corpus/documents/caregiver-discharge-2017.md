# Caregiver integration during discharge planning of older adults

- ID: caregiver-discharge-2017

## Evidence

The meta-analysis found lower readmission when caregivers were integrated into discharge planning: RR 0.75 (95% CI 0.62 to 0.91) at 90 days and RR 0.76 (0.64 to 0.90) at 180 days.

## Modelling use and caveats

This is evidence for a discharge-planning intervention, not evidence for the probability of routing to community care or the time required to process a discharge letter.

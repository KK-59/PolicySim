# Trends in clinical workload in UK primary care, 2005 to 2019

- ID: gp-workload-trends-2024
- Publisher: British Journal of General Practice
- Year: 2024
- Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11388090/
- Parameters: capacities.gpAdminShare
- Range or CI: yes
- Evidence kind: literature
- Applicability: direct

## Evidence

Age-standardised GP administrative workload rose from 4.60 minutes per person-year in 2005 to 12.53 in 2019, while total GP workload rose from 28.31 to 42.36 minutes. Dividing administrative by total GP workload gives observed shares of approximately 16.3% and 29.6%.

## Modelling use and caveats

The 16.3% to 29.6% range is a transparent derivation across two historical years, not a confidence interval or a cross-practice distribution. It is suitable as a broad literature prior for gpAdminShare, with this limitation visible.

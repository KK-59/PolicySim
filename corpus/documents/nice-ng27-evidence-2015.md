## Evidence

The evidence review reports that early discharge planning reduced readmission, with a risk ratio of 0.78 and 95% confidence interval 0.69 to 0.90 in the cited synthesis.

## Modelling use and caveats

This is an intervention effect on readmission, not the fraction routed to community care and not a document-processing time. It can parameterise a separately modelled discharge-planning effect only.

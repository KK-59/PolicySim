## Evidence

The stocktake proposes integrated neighbourhood teams, streamlined urgent same-day access and more personalised continuity for people with complex needs. It describes multidisciplinary routing across primary, community, mental-health, pharmacy and voluntary services.

## Modelling use and caveats

The document supports the available policy pathways but does not estimate referral probabilities. It must not be used to fill the routing fields numerically.
